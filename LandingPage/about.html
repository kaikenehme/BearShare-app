About BearShare

BearShare transforms every wildlife encounter into a beautiful, shareable digital card—empowering you to explore, collect, and contribute to conservation efforts around the globe.

Capture & Create

Snap a photo, and be rewarded with a hand-drawn, detailed species card with facts and imagery.

Share & Contribute

Your logged sightings help build a global wildlife database used by researchers and conservationists.

Collect & Reward

Unlock badges, milestones, and special rewards as your digital card collection grows.
